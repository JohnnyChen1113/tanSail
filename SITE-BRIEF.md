## Original direction

### Principles

- Separate verified technology facts from illustrative template content
- Let visitors scan compatibility and scenarios before reading detailed proof
- Keep every module reusable through typed configuration and bilingual content

### Materials

- Opaque editorial cards on the existing Luminous Harbor canvas
- Original lettermarks, semantic accent tones, hairline borders, and restrained shadows
- Compact filter pills reserved for interaction rather than decoration

### Product proof

- Technology names verified from the TanSail dependency and deployment configuration
- Scenario descriptions bounded by the six maintained recipes and browser-only core
- Testimonials visibly marked as replaceable sample content until verified feedback exists

## Required transformations

- Replace every third-party logo with an original typographic lettermark and verified technology label
- Use a responsive semantic grid and rail instead of reproducing either reference logo layout
- Turn the tool directory into TanSail site-scenario filters with original categories and copy
- Use an accessible three-column card flow with explicit sample badges instead of presenting fabricated social proof
- Place the modules inside TanSail's hero-to-delivery narrative rather than following the reference section order

## Clean-room rule

Do not copy source code, copy, brand assets, or screenshots. Rebuild only the observed design
principles with TanSail primitives, original content, original assets, and a visibly distinct
composition. Record any third-party asset and its license before it enters the repository.
